2. Physical SQLite Denormalization Rationale & Field Mapping

In the production runtime (pnp_checkpoint.db), entities MOTORIST, VEHICLE, and OFFICER are denormalized directly into the central violations table (20 columns). This architectural decision eliminates multi-table join latency in the field, prevents foreign key locks during high-throughput checkpoint stops, allows instantaneous single-statement parameterized transactions (INSERT INTO violations), and enables offline SQLite standalone operation on ruggedized field laptops.
